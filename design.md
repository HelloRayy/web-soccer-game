# Design System: Cyber Mint Teal & Deep Obsidian ⚽⚡

Panduan Design System untuk **Web Soccer Game**, terinspirasi dari **Futuristic Valorant Neon Mint Teal UI** (`#17FFBF` / `#00F5B8`).

---

## 1. Color Palette (Cyber Mint & Deep Navy)

### Background & Surface
- **Primary Page BG**: `#060D17` (Deep Cyber Obsidian)
- **Container BG**: `#0A1526` (Cyber Navy Turf)
- **Border Line**: `#142840` (Subtle Steel Blue Border)

### Primary Accent Colors
- ⚡ **Electric Mint Teal**: `#17FFBF` / `#00F5B8` (Primary Active Button, FC26 Logo Accent, Focus Brackets)
- 🩵 **Cyber Cyan**: `#00D8F6` (Secondary Accents, Telemetry Status)
- 🏆 **Champions Gold**: `#FFD13B` (Trophy Badges, Highlights)
- 🖤 **Dark Cyber Text**: `#060D17` (High-Contrast Text di atas tombol Mint Teal)

---

## 2. Button Specs

- **Active Button Fill**: Electric Mint Teal `#17FFBF`
- **Active Button Text**: Deep Cyber Navy `#060D17` `font-black`
- **Border**: Sharp `#17FFBF` (1.5px)
- **Hover Transition**: `#00F5B8` / `#50FFCF`
